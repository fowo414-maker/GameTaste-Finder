What is GameTaste Finder?

A static recommendation site that finds the next game worth playing based on games you've liked. Select several games to surface what they have in common, and see the results right on screen.

Built to load light, compare fast, and lead straight into a recommendation.

Selecting games

Selecting more games helps us capture the common thread in your taste more clearly. One click toggles the selection, so you can immediately see which games are shaping the criteria.

The more games you pick, the sharper the recommendation criteria become.

Tag-based recommendations

Even when the genre is the same, the real feel of play shows up more clearly in the tags. Comparing tags like Open World, Co-op, Story, and Survival makes it easier to find games closer to your actual taste.

Captures finer differences in taste even within a similar genre.

Recommendation algorithm

We compare genre, mood, and play style together to calculate a recommendation ranking. Because it weighs several axes rather than just one, the results reflect your taste better than a simple list would.

The same game can rank differently depending on which criteria led you to pick it.
